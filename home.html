Waste Categories

🍎

Organic Waste

Green Bin

Organic waste is any material originating from living organisms that is biodegradable and can be broken down by microorganisms into simpler substances.

  • Food scraps
  • Fruit peels
  • Vegetable peels
  • Tea bag
♻️

Recyclable Waste

Blue Bin

Recyclable waste refers to materials that can be collected, processed, and repurposed into new products instead of being discarded as trash.

  • Paper
  • Plastic bottles
  • Glass bottles
  • Metal cans
☣️

Hazardous Waste

Red Bin

Hazardous waste can be solid, liquid, or gaseous and is considered dangerous because it can cause harm if improperly handled, stored, or disposed.

  • Batteries
  • Medicines
  • Paint
  • Chemicals
🗑️

Residual Waste

Black Bin

Residual waste consists of materials that cannot be reused, recycled, or composted and are usually sent to landfills.

  • Diapers
  • Tissues
  • Dirty plastic
  • Wrappers
